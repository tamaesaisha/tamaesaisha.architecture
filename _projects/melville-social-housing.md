<p>The Melville project explores housing as a framework for everyday life, focusing on how space can support both living and community. The design introduces public spaces between alleyways, transforming one into a mixed-use parking area and the other into a shared seating garden, creating active thresholds between adjacent social housing.</p>
<p>These spaces connect to shared programmes, including workshops focused on skills development and workers’ rights, allowing residents to engage in activities that support both learning and economic opportunity, culminating in a weekend market.</p>
