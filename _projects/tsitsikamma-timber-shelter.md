<p>Exploring different forms through a series of 1:100 concept models inspired the idea of a walk-on roof. Through these models I became drawn to height and movement — how the body relates to the fall of water.</p>
<p>The 1:50 process model refined this, exploring a walk-on roof that lets users experience the landscape and water from above, with a view of the waterfall. The 1:25 model explored the balance between structure and landscape, translating the natural slopes and materials into a compact, experiential form.</p>
