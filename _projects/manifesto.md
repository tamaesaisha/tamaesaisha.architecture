<p>I believe meaningful spaces are not defined solely by their form, but by the experiences, emotions, and interactions they enable. My work is driven by an interest in atmosphere. How does light, material, sound, texture, and movement influence the way people perceive and inhabit space?</p>
<p>I approach architecture as the design of ecosystems rather than isolated objects. Buildings exist within larger networks of people, cultures, landscapes, climates, and urban systems. As a designer, I am interested in understanding these relationships and creating environments that contribute positively to the social and ecological fabric of a place.</p>
<p>Materiality plays a central role in my design process. I am fascinated by the way materials communicate character, memory, craft, and permanence. Whether through the solidity of masonry, the lightness of steel, or the tactile qualities of natural materials, I strive to create architecture that can be experienced through both the eye and the body.</p>
<p>Key principles: human experience; materiality and atmosphere; ecological thinking; connection and community; curiosity and play.</p>
