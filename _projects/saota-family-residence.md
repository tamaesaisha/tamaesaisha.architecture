<p>During a student internship at SAOTA, I designed a bespoke family residence on a steep coastal site in Budva, Montenegro, working one-on-one with a SAOTA architect from concept to presentation.</p>
<p>The project began with sketches studying the local vernacular, then developed in Revit as a coordinated BIM model with full documentation. Visualisations were produced in Twinmotion alongside AI-assisted rendering workflows.</p>
